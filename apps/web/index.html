01 — The shift

The blue links are going dark.

For twenty years, being found meant ranking. Ten blue links, a click, your site. That contract is quietly breaking — people stopped searching and started asking, and the answer arrives already written, drawn from a handful of sources that may or may not be you.

When a machine answers instead of linking, you are either in the answer or invisible. There is no page two. There is no page one. There is one paragraph and a few citations — and everything rides on whether one of them is yours.

If you're not in the answer, you don't exist.

02 — The measurement lie

A single score is a comfortable lie.

Ask a model the same question five times and you'll get five different answers. It cites you once, forgets you twice, misquotes you the rest. So any tool that hands you a confident “73.4” is inventing a precision the machine itself doesn't have.

So we don't guess once. We poll every engine, many times, and report the band — a number you can act on precisely because it admits what it doesn't know. That honesty is the whole point of the readout above.

03 — Why we exist

Measuring the wound is not healing it.

The market is crowded with beautiful dashboards that tell you you're losing. Almost none of them fix anything.

Knowing your schema is broken, your pages un-citable, the AI crawlers blocked at your own front door — and then doing the repair by hand, page by page, across a whole site — is exactly where every team drowns. Diagnosis is cheap now. Execution is the moat.

04 — What we're building

See it, fix it, watch it move.

Engine closes the loop. It measures your visibility honestly, diagnoses exactly what's holding you back, then deploys the fix — JSON-LD, meta, crawler policy — straight to your site or as a pull request, staged and reversible. Then it re-measures, to prove the number actually moved.

No more exporting a to-do list to a developer who's already underwater. The insight and the repair live in the same place, and the loop keeps turning.
