What Engine does

Visibility

Where you stand across search results and AI answers — as an honest range, never a false-precise number.

Rank Tracking

Early access

Knowing where you rank in Google — across every market, device and language you care about, and the moment it moves.

AI Answer Visibility

Early access

Answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overviews — increasingly decide what people discover.

Unified Visibility Score

Available

Rank tracking, AI visibility and local each answer a fragment of one question: how discoverable are you?

Keyword & Prompt Research

Available

Before you can track anything you have to decide what to track — both classic keywords and the prompts people actually type into AI engines.

Competitor Intelligence

Available

Visibility is relative — the useful question is where competitors beat you, and why.

Backlinks & Brand Mentions

Available

For AI visibility, unlinked brand mentions carry more signal than backlinks — but most tooling indexes links and ignores mentions.

Diagnosis

What is holding you back, and the specific change that resolves it.

Technical Audit

Available

Engine reads your site the way a browser does, finds what is technically wrong, and — the part that matters — gives you problems that can actually be fixed rather than a list of warnings.

Content & Extractability

Available

Whether an AI engine quotes you depends largely on whether your page is easy to lift an answer out of: answers up front, passages that stand on their own, real coverage of the subject, credible signs of expertise.

Entity & Knowledge Graph

Available

AI engines and Google's Knowledge Graph reason about businesses, not pages.

Crawl Analysis

Coming soon

Local Visibility

Available
