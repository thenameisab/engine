What Engine does

Engine is built in pillars. Everything in Visibility exists to feed Diagnosis, and every diagnosis is written to become an executable fix — that contract was frozen on day one.

Pillar A — Visibility

Measure where you stand across search results and AI answers, as an honest range.

A1 Partial

Rank Tracking

Knowing where you rank in Google — across every market, device and language you care about, and the moment it moves.

MVP · Scoring live; awaiting a SERP data provider for live ingestion
A2 Partial

AI Visibility (GEO)

Answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overviews — increasingly decide what people discover.

MVP · Adapters live; awaiting per-engine LLM API access
A3 Shipped

Unified Share of Voice (Unified Visibility Score)

Rank tracking, AI visibility and local each answer a fragment of one question: how discoverable are you?

MVP · Unified Visibility Score with confidence bands
A4 Shipped

Keyword & Prompt Research

Before you can track anything you have to decide what to track — both classic keywords and the prompts people actually type into AI engines.

MVP · MVP slice — packages/keywords

Competitor Intelligence

v1.5 · packages/competitor

Backlink & Mention Index
